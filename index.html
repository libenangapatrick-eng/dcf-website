OUR STORY

Compassion Into Action

Development Charity Foundation (DCF) is a Tanzanian non-profit, non-religious and non-political organization established in 2022 in Dodoma, Tanzania.

DCF began as a youth-led initiative with 8 members and has grown to more than 57 active members.

Our work focuses on humanitarian support, education, youth empowerment, health, environmental protection, economic empowerment and community development.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a trusted and exceptional institution contributing to sustainable development, community welfare and improved quality of life for vulnerable and underserved communities in Tanzania and beyond.

Our Mission

To empower communities through humanitarian support, education, health services, economic opportunities, advocacy, environmental initiatives and sustainable development.
