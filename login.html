2. Rules, Rights & Responsibilities
Read the key membership requirements before confirmation.
  1. Membership is open to a person aged 18 years or above who accepts DCF's objectives, vision, mission, values and Constitution.
  2. DCF does not discriminate on the grounds stated in its Constitution, subject to applicable law.
  3. An applicant must complete the official application, provide legally recognised identification, accept the Constitution, Rules and Ethics, pay approved joining fees where applicable, and attend orientation when required.
  4. Membership rights begin after formal approval, entry in the membership register and fulfilment of the applicable basic requirements.
  5. Members are expected to respect the Constitution and lawful decisions, protect DCF's name and property, keep institutional information confidential, participate in activities, pay approved dues on time, avoid corruption, discrimination, harassment and abuse of authority, and provide truthful information.
  6. A member may be suspended for serious disciplinary investigation, prolonged failure to perform duties without valid reason, or breach of the Constitution/Rules; no suspension should occur without an opportunity to be heard.
  7. Membership may end by written resignation, lawful expulsion, death, loss of eligibility, or a serious offence that damages the Foundation's reputation, subject to the Constitution.
  8. A member affected by suspension or expulsion has a right of appeal to the General Meeting within 30 days of receiving the decision.
  9. Disciplinary procedures provide for notice, adequate time to prepare a defence, an opportunity to present evidence, and a fair hearing. Sanctions depend on the seriousness of the offence.
  10. Members and leaders must disclose conflicts of interest and withdraw from decisions where they have a personal or close-family interest.
